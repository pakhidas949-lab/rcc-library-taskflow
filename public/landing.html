apartment About the library

“A library is not a luxury but one of the necessities of life.”

— H. G. Wells

The RCC IIT Central Library was conceived as the shared intellectual infrastructure of the Institute. It supports undergraduate and postgraduate teaching, doctoral research and the continuing education programmes run by every academic department.

Its collections are developed jointly with the departments, and its services are designed around the real workflow of study — from discovering a source to submitting a thesis. The staff portal below is where librarians manage circulation, acquisitions and the patron record.
